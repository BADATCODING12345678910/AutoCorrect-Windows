Privacy Policy

This policy describes how AutoCorrect for Windows handles information. It matches how the desktop app and this website actually work today.

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · AutoCorrect Lab (autocorrectlab@outlook.com)

1. Short version

  • No account is required to download or use AutoCorrect.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or product analytics SDKs in the app.
  • Most typing stays on your PC. Text only leaves your device when you use a feature that needs a network service (explained below).
  • Privacy mode (tray) blocks cloud Fix / Rewrite for a timed period or until you turn it off.

2. What AutoCorrect does on your computer

AutoCorrect is a local Windows tray app. To correct text in other apps it uses accessibility APIs (UI Automation), keyboard hooks / hotkeys, and sometimes the clipboard. That processing happens on your machine so the product can read and replace text in the field you are using.

Settings, learned corrections, dictionaries, snippets, themes, and optional API keys are stored under:

%LocalAppData%\AutoCorrect\

Your optional AI / LLM key is saved locally in PersonalApiKey.ini in that folder (bring-your-own-key). We do not host your key on our servers.

3. When text or other data leaves your device

Depending on features you enable, AutoCorrect may send:

  • LanguageTool (default Fix path). When Fix uses LanguageTool, the text being checked is sent to api.languagetool.org (LanguageTool’s public API) to get grammar/spelling suggestions. Their terms and privacy policy apply to that request.
  • Optional AI providers (Perfect Fix / Rewrite / writing suggestions). If you paste your own API key and enable AI features, the relevant text (and your key in the Authorization header) is sent to the provider you chose in Settings → Grammar (for example OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic, Groq, DeepSeek, or a custom base URL). Those providers process the request under their privacy policies. We do not receive a copy of that traffic.
  • In-app updates. Checking for updates contacts our update feed (Supabase Edge Function update-feed) to learn whether a newer version exists (version metadata / download URL / checksum). That check does not include the contents of documents you are typing.
  • Language packs. If you select a non-bundled language, pack files may download from our website (Cloudflare Pages, with a GitHub Pages mirror as fallback).
  • Support tickets. If you submit the website Support form, the fields you enter (name/email, message, optional app/Windows version) are sent to our support backend so we can reply.
  • Donations. Optional tips open PayPal checkout (via our donate helper, then PayPal). Payment details are handled by PayPal; we may see that a tip completed and the amount, not your full card numbers.

4. Privacy mode

Tray → Privacy lets you turn on Privacy mode for a set number of minutes or until you end it. While active, cloud Fix providers and Rewrite are blocked so those features stay local-only / disabled. The active privacy session (end time / until-off) is stored locally in privacy-session.json so it can survive an app restart.

5. What we do not collect by default

  • No mandatory AutoCorrect user account or login wall.
  • No advertising ID, crash-telemetry vendor, or marketing pixel in the desktop app.
  • We do not upload your full typing history to “train a global model” for us.
  • Learned typo pairs and personal dictionary entries stay on your PC unless you deliberately export a backup or include them in a support message.

6. This website

The marketing site (Cloudflare Pages primary; GitHub Pages is a noindex fallback mirror) is mostly static HTML. It may store small preferences in your browser (localStorage for light/dark theme and a site-version soft-refresh flag). Those are not used to track you across other sites. We do not use third-party ad cookies on these pages.

7. Children

AutoCorrect is a general productivity tool for Windows. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). Do not submit personal information about a child through Support.

8. Third parties

Services that may process data when you use related features:

  • LanguageTool (grammar API)
  • Your chosen LLM provider (only if you enable AI + provide a key)
  • Supabase (update feed / support / donate helpers we operate)
  • PayPal (donations)
  • Cloudflare / GitHub (website and installer hosting)

9. Your choices

  • Use LanguageTool-only, AI, or local rules via Settings → Grammar.
  • Turn on Privacy mode to block cloud Fix / Rewrite temporarily.
  • Clear or omit an API key anytime; delete files under %LocalAppData%\AutoCorrect\ to wipe local app data.
  • Uninstall the app via Windows Settings or the Setup uninstaller.
  • Contact us to ask about a support ticket you submitted.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page on the official site: https://autocorrect-windows-site.pages.dev/privacy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: autocorrectlab@outlook.com or the Support form.
